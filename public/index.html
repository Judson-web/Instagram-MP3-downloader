GOOD TO KNOW

A few quick answers.

Which links are supported?+

Public Instagram Reels and video post links are supported. Private, removed, or login-gated videos may not be accessible.

Do I need to upload a video or sign in?+

No file upload and no Instagram login are required. ReelTone processes the link on its conversion service and does not ask for your Instagram password.

Why didn't a link convert?+

Instagram availability can change. Check that the link is correct and public, then try again. Some links may be temporarily unavailable to the converter.
