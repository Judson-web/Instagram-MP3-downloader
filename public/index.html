GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered.

Which links are supported?+

Public Instagram Reels and video post links are supported. Private, removed, or login-gated videos may not be accessible.

Do I need to upload a video or sign in?+

No file upload or Instagram login is required. ReelTone fetches audio through its own Vercel conversion function. If Instagram doesn't expose media directly, the public Reel URL is sent to a third-party link resolver to obtain the public media URL. Never enter your Instagram password here.

How are private or login-gated Reels handled?+

When Instagram explicitly identifies a Reel as private or login-required, ReelTone shows that specific status. Sometimes Instagram only returns “no media”; in that case the app says no public video was found instead of guessing whether the post is private, removed, or temporarily restricted.

Why didn't a link convert?+

Instagram availability and anonymous request limits can change. Check the link, wait a few minutes if the source is rate-limiting requests, and try again. Repeated retries won't make private or login-gated media public.
